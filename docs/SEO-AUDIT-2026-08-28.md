# TGPI SEO Audit — 2026-08-28

## Original findings

- Production redirects the apex domain to `www`, while root metadata, sitemap and parts of JSON-LD used the apex hostname.
- The sitemap contained a certificates route that declared `noindex`.
- Protected and user-generated route families did not share a central index policy.
- Homepage schemas used inconsistent entity IDs and included FAQ markup without a material Google rich-result benefit for this site type.
- Country pages had strong visual coverage and route discovery but highly repeated editorial structures and no source/review registry.
- All 195 country URLs were included in the sitemap regardless of content maturity.
- Compare query states were useful product states but no controlled editorial comparison URL system existed.
- Several institutional pages inherited generic root metadata.
- The route described as methodology presented product-value marketing instead of a real research methodology.
- No internal search route connected country, course and institutional entities.

## Implemented response

- Central canonical origin set to `https://www.theglobalpolymath.com`.
- Typed metadata, robots, URL and schema helpers added under `src/seo/`.
- Organization, WebSite, Founder, Country, Course and Breadcrumb entities connected through stable IDs.
- Sitemap now uses a central public policy and a conservative 19-country quality gate.
- Private, search, user-generated and authenticated route families explicitly noindexed.
- Founder, Editorial Policy, Contact and internal Search pages created.
- Methodology page rewritten around evidence, trade-offs, uncertainty and limitations.
- Public course overview separated from protected course lessons.
- Country metadata now targets cost, work, study and moving intent while retaining a single canonical page.
- Search, footer and share URLs standardized to the canonical host.
- Automated SEO verification and operational documentation added.

## Deferred intentionally

- Search Console verification token and production submission.
- Multilingual URLs and `hreflang`.
- Standalone country-intent pages.
- Permanent editorial comparison routes.
- Mass ranking hubs.
- Indexing the remaining country profiles.

These require real content, sources, review data or production authority and must not be synthesized merely to expand URL count.
