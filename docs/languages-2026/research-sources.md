# TGPI language expansion — source review

Reviewed 21 November 2026. This document records reference research, not independent verification of every TGPI lesson or phrase. Course authors must validate each target-language sentence, register, script and translation before claiming expert or native-speaker review. Linking a resource does not imply endorsement, accreditation, partnership or permission to reproduce it.

## Evidence and measurement

The research did not establish a comparable worldwide ranking of **2026 graduates of language courses**. Demand, app popularity, enrolments, test sessions, speakers and graduates are different measures. Do not combine their counts or label 2024/2025 observations as 2026 observations. A publication issued in 2026 can report an earlier measurement period. November 2026 also precedes the end of the year.

Editorial selection: prioritize the ten globally popular languages above, then add regionally useful access paths (Arabic, Swahili, Indonesian, Turkish, Russian, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Vietnamese and Thai). Providing ten scenario courses per language is a product architecture choice, not a claim that demand is equal across languages.

## Publication implications

- Author original TGPI texts and exercises. Free-to-read does not mean commercially reusable; references can remain external links.
- Preserve target-language utterances independently of interface translation. Use correct BCP 47 language tags for speech/audio, screen readers and RTL handling. Device text-to-speech is a practice aid; it is neither a native-speaker recording nor verified pronunciation scoring.
- A short thematic course can deliver a specific communication outcome without establishing comprehensive A1/A2/B1 proficiency. Keep assessed scope and certificate wording aligned with actual evidence.
- Do not award or advertise official IELTS, TOEFL, Goethe, DELE, DELF, JLPT, TOPIK or Celpe-Bras equivalence without the corresponding external assessment and authorization.
- A cover generated as an illustrative scene is not proof of a campus, teacher, partnership or learner result. Photorealistic style does not change its provenance.
