# TGPI language laboratories — 2026 expansion

Selection reviewed on 21 November 2026. This release adds **200 thematic introductory laboratories: 20 target languages × 10 communication contexts**. It does not represent 200 complete language-to-fluency programmes. Equal numbers per language are a curriculum-design decision; they are not a claim of equal market demand.

## Selection

The first ten target languages follow the broad set prominent in Duolingo's 2025 global report: English, Spanish, French, Japanese, German, Korean, Italian, Chinese, Portuguese and Hindi. The catalogue order groups coherent pathways rather than reproducing a statistical ranking. Arabic, Swahili, Indonesian, Turkish, Russian, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Vietnamese and Thai broaden geographic and practical coverage; their inclusion is editorial, not a claim that these are the remaining ten largest markets.

The research distinguishes publication year, observation period and measure. No comparable worldwide ranking of language-course graduates in 2026 was established. The year is not complete at the review date. Enrolments, course seats, active app learners and graduates must not be added together.

## Learning and assessment

Each laboratory has eight lessons of 30 minutes estimated autonomous work, a 90-minute portfolio and a 30-minute assessment allowance: six hours total. Learners practice a limited set of expressions and topic vocabulary through reading, retrieval, writing, listening when local device voices are available, communication repair and scenario transfer. Time estimates are not video durations or measured guarantees.

Each course has 24 private assessment items. Final and delayed review select one essential item from each lesson plus eight additional items; options and question identifiers are shuffled on the server. Language assessments do not append an unrelated numerical exercise. Lesson evidence, portfolio, final, delayed review, entitlement and verified identity continue through the existing Academy service. The certificate records completion of the laboratory; it does not establish full CEFR proficiency or official examination equivalence. Writing and speech practice are not automatically graded for linguistic quality.

Target-language text retains its own BCP 47 tag and direction independently of Portuguese instruction and the six interface locales. Modern Standard Arabic uses RTL. Hindi/Polish/Portuguese examples include explicit gender/register notes. No generated voice is called a native-speaker recording or a pronunciation score. Only a compatible local device voice is selected; no new paid speech or AI service is introduced.

## Artwork and publication

Every course has an individually generated photographic illustration, optimized as a 1600-pixel-wide WebP and used consistently across catalogue, overview and classroom. Fictional images do not establish a TGPI campus, teacher or partnership. [artwork-jobs.json](artwork-jobs.json) retains distinct briefs; asset manifests record generation provenance. Exact file hashes and normalized thumbnail hashes must be unique before release.

Publication requires all 200 images, language content/assessment checks, the existing regression suite, localization audit, type check and production build. A preview-launch failure caused by Vite-specific flags supplied to the existing Next.js dev command is an environment verification limitation; the application dev command is preserved. Interactive browser QA is not represented as completed on the basis of source checks.
